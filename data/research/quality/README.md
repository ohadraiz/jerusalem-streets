# Story-grade highlights

`highlights.json` lists 200 cards that read as stories rather than dictionary entries: they carry a year, a dramatic event, and/or a named building, and they are long enough to breathe (110–260 characters).

## Score

| Signal | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Contains a year (1000–2029) | +3 |
| Contains any number | +2 |
| Dramatic verb (נהרג, נבנה, הוקם, גורש, הציל, נסגר …) | +2 |
| A named place or institution (בית, מגדל, מנזר, כנסייה, שוק, יקב, מוזיאון …) | +2 |
| Length 110–260 characters | +1 |
| The whole card is only "<street> is named after X." | −4 |

Distribution across all 1,305 cards: 69 cards score 9–10, 169 score 8, 75 score 7, and 266 score 6. The 200 listed here are the ones at 7 and above.

## How to use it

- As a curation list: these are the cards to show first, tweet, or put in a "מומלץ" rail.
- As a backlog signal: cards scoring 0–2 (about 380) are candidates for replacement rather than rewriting, usually because the namesake link itself is weak.

## What is not here

No app code and no schema change. The list is a research artifact; wiring a "highlight" flag into the app is a separate, approved change.
